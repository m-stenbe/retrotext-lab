# R09 Daina and Begi editorial review

The two R09 editorial packs contain 29 script records in five connected scene
groups and the Daina map label. They cover the Daina village and services,
memorial reunion and ambush, escape aftermath, the Begi historian’s complete
book-fetch and history sequence, and party reactions before departure for Stea.
The military-station refusal after Lucia joins is included. Optional dialogue,
repeat inspection, services and inventory-full branches remain alternatives.

Canonical English was written from the original Japanese and checked as a
continuous RPG script. It preserves Lucia’s simultaneous identity, uncertainty
about the Tiryut’s origins, the reported relationship between Ralua and Karma,
Welda’s skepticism, and the historian’s attributed account of persecution in
Dumna’s name. It does not turn those speakers’ claims into independent facts.
Existing Myuntos, encyclopedia and Trefoil Emblem terminology is retained;
new provisional names and their evidence are recorded in the manager-owned bible.
Independent QA corrected the expedition question’s runtime name from Karu to
Jido before the scene review fingerprints were regenerated.

The separate adaptation preserves every original command and runtime name.
Narration and dialogue use guarded continuation pages. The scholar header call
has a source-checked default-attribute effect; the colored encyclopedia-title
call retains its original reset and is validated within the complete request
and retrieval. The five-line memorial inscription retains its original controls.
Full canonical text is unrestricted. Record-specific notes document compact
labels, omitted forms of address, simplified narration and other material
fitting decisions; MARS HISTORY names the full encyclopedia in its narrow
composed layout.

The complete R08 baseline plus all 29 scripts compiled successfully after the
shared-header support was added. The Daina label uses the guarded location
adapter. Final cumulative release-gate and preservation checks belong to the
manager and independent QA. Runtime traversal, continuation-page appearance and
player editorial feedback remain pending; a PR does not require prior player
verification.
