# R05 Saibal connected-scene editorial review

The Saibal pack contains 50 new canonical records in six connected scene groups:
street conversations, shops and hotel outcomes, syndicate confrontation,
kidnapping, underground bar inquiries and party reactions. Canonical English was
written from the Japanese and source-controlled branch context, then read in
connected English before separate fitting adaptations were prepared.

RE established the map9 departure to map53 Saibal, the guard battle, interrogation
flag17 and hotel kidnapping flag18. The later flag19 victory barks and flag26
wake-up are outside this packet. Street conversations and TALK alternatives are
not presented as a mandatory linear sequence. The party caller headers establish
Shoko for081000:003, Joe for004/006 and Welda for005/007.

Porkin remains a provisional source-based place name. Existing working names for
Saibal, Zajil, Chausnomyu, Tomyu and Kidun are preserved. The Myuntos speaker's
replaceable right-arm gun is described without inventing biological details.
Welda's claim that she can legally break the syndicate members' arms stays an
attributed threat. Her later gruesome outburst remains an intention, not an event.
The kidnappers' polite letter masks coercion; Joe and Welda argue afterward until
Sion insists that rescuing Shoko comes first.

Independent QA prompted two canonical improvements: the syndicate warning uses
“his days are numbered” for the source death-threat idiom; Tomyu is to be “hauled
in,” expressing the arrest context. Adaptations were checked again against these
revisions and rebound to the resulting review fingerprints.

Fitting retains the original commands, runtime-name sequence and allocations.
Explicit continuation pages are balanced at meaningful phrase boundaries. Shop
choices retain their original two rows. The kidnapping letter keeps two original
five-row pages with explicit newlines: its constrained version preserves the
addressee, welcome, nuisance motive, hostage, meeting at Porkin and warning, while
omitting the repeated planet name and euphemism about taking the girl into care.
All material shortening is documented in the adaptation pack.

Source, allocation and composed-layout compilation are integration checks;
full route traversal, visual appearance, branch order and naturalness in gameplay
remain runtime verification work. Full original source and reading catalogs stay
in ignored work files.
