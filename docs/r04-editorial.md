# R04 editorial handoff

The new dialogue comprises **41 records in six connected scenes**, covering the
Atraia distress call, boarding and hostage rescue, compelled station induction,
the complete initial tour and its alternatives, and the first mission briefing.
Conditional party responses and four conservative bridge lines are included;
those bridge lines do not extend the promised route beyond the station boundary.
Canonical English and technical adaptations are separate deliverables:

- `profiles/alshark/editorial-r04-rescue.json`
- `profiles/alshark/adaptations-r04-rescue.json`
- `profiles/alshark/editorial-r04-station.json`
- `profiles/alshark/adaptations-r04-station.json`

Each canonical scene was read continuously against the original decoded source,
including the approach warning, shared speaker calls, question alternatives,
flag-dependent repeats and first briefing. The existing R03 script was context,
not a translation template. The adaptation was then authored separately and
mechanically wrapped against the immutable names and control boundaries.

The rescue preserves the interrupted name and immediate self-correction, the
uncertainty in the soldier's remark about Mamon's changed instincts, Shoko's doubt,
Mamon's claim that his mission justified killing Jido, Joe's anger, and Shayna's
company boast. The hostage sequence distinguishes the gun against Shayna's throat,
the shot through her captor's arm, her being shoved toward Welda, and Karu's head
being shot off. Welda is consistently a woman. Neither the identity suggested by
the interrupted name nor any explanation of Mamon's behavior is invented.

The induction retains Joe Compson's first full-name disclosure, Roy Bester's rank,
the threat against the unregistered ship and crew, and the minder/babysitter joke.
Chausnomyu denotes an organization, whose acronym is explained in both question
outcomes; it is not treated as a planet. The briefing preserves Kidun's addiction
and hallucinations, biojunk's genetic mutation and internal/external changes,
usual death, the rare bio-beast outcome, the recent Federation massacres, and all
three dead agents. Karilas, Tomyu, Myuntos, Zajil and the Saibal directions remain
explicit. Zajil matches the already reviewed ticket spelling.

Canonical prose retains details that need compression in the game. Per-record
adaptation notes identify these, including the reactor's miniature size and turbine
mechanism, some movement and facial-description detail, the opening alarm's exact
timing, and the Secretary header abbreviation `SEC`. Names and organization terms
remain provisional where the bible says so. The manager owns their bible records
and guarded name patches.

Long narration uses the reviewed R04 wait/clear/restore-narration continuation
adapter. Original commands, source page boundaries, runtime names and opaque
suffixes remain protected. Shared headers are measured together with their
callers. Independent QA reviewed canonical fidelity, then requested corrections to
uncertainty, troop/gear comparison, natural short English and action referents;
those corrections were applied before the final fitting check.

Validation against original images reports all **41 new dialogue records passing
source, editorial, allocation and composed-layout checks**. This is an editorial
and static-fitting result. Full runtime traversal, speaker presentation, timing,
branch interaction and naturalness in play remain candidate verification work.
