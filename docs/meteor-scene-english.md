# Meteor-site English reading guide

The arrival and aftermath below are included in build 06. This cinematic reading
translation is provided for following the story; cinematic text in the game is
still Japanese. Names use the project's provisional spellings. This guide has
story spoilers for the complete meteor-site sequence.

## Cinematic: before Jido's conversation

The canyon's meteor crash site lay silent. Sion and Shoko hid behind the rocks
and watched. The survey team, including both their fathers, should have been
there. But alongside the team's jet hovercraft, an unfamiliar spacecraft had
landed.

**Shoko:** What's that? A Federation military ship? I've never seen that type before.

**Sion:** No way. That's a Zolias Empire combat craft!

**Shoko:** Zolias...? What are they doing here?!

**Sion:** Shh! Someone's coming out.

The spacecraft's ramp opened silently. The two watched with bated breath.

**Shoko:** Father... Sion! They've captured our fathers!

**Sion:** Quiet! If they spot us, we'll be captured too.

**Shoko:** But... Sion, can't we do something? If we leave them like this,
your father will be killed too!

**Sion:** Calm down, Shoko. We don't know that they're going to kill them.

**Shoko:** But...

A gunshot suddenly rang out. Startled, the two turned back. Seizing a momentary
lapse in the soldiers' attention, Jido and the others had attacked the Zolias
commander. Jido struck the commander hard in the forehead, sending him sprawling.
Dr. Penrose rushed in to seize the gun in his hand. But the moment the doctor
touched it, something happened.

**Dr. Penrose:** Aaaaaargh!

**Jido:** Mamon!

**Jido:** Mamon... Are you all right...?

**Dr. Penrose:** ...Hmph. Not bad...

**Jido:** Hey, Mamon! What's wrong?

**Dr. Penrose:** ...Kill them!

Three merciless gunshots echoed through the canyon. Sion and Shoko could not
even cry out. Dr. Penrose, now seemingly an entirely different person, barked an
order.

**Dr. Penrose:** Hurry! Alshark can't have gone far!

Sion: Dad! DAAAD! Damn it! Those Zolias soldiers... I'll never forgive them!

Shoko: S... Sion... I don't know what to say. But please don't hate my father. There must be some explanation...
